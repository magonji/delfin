How the Budget works

Overview
Set a monthly budget and track your spending against it. The progress bar shows how much of your budget you've used.
Key figures
Spent — Total from actual transactions this month (excluding transfers).
Committed — Recurring and planned expenses still pending payment.
Available — Budget minus spent minus committed.
Recurring expenses
Recurring expenses are automatically detected as paid when a matching transaction is found (same payee, similar amount).
✓ Paid — matching transaction found
○ Pending — not yet paid, counts as committed
✕ Skipped — manually marked as "won't pay this month"
— N/A — doesn't apply this month (quarterly, annual, etc.)
Click ○ to skip an expense this month (removes it from committed). Click ✕ to undo.
Planned expenses
One-time expenses expected this month. Click the status icon to toggle between paid and pending.
Other expenses
All transactions this month that didn't match a recurring expense, grouped by week.
